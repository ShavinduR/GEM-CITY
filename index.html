How buying works

Simple, transparent, fully insured

Buying a precious stone from the other side of the world should feel as safe as buying it in person. Here is how we make sure it does.

  1. Choose your stone

    Browse the collection or tell us the colour, size and budget you have in mind. We also source to order.

  2. See it live

    We show you the actual stone on a video call in natural light, together with its original lab report.

    Only once you are happy: bank transfer or card, with Koko and Mintpay instalments in Sri Lanka.

  4. Insured delivery

    FedEx or DHL, insured and tracked to your door, backed by our 14-day money-back guarantee.

Questions

Buying with confidence

Is it safe to buy a gemstone online from Sri Lanka?

Yes, and we make it verifiable. Before you pay, we show you the actual stone on a live video call in natural light, together with its original gem lab report. Every parcel is shipped fully insured with tracking, and you are covered by our 14-day money-back guarantee.

Are your gems natural and certified?

Every stone we sell is natural and comes with a gemmological report. For premium stones we provide reports from internationally recognised laboratories such as NGJA (Sri Lanka), GIA or GRS. The report for each stone is shown in its listing and on request before purchase.

How does international shipping work?

We ship worldwide with FedEx or DHL, fully insured and tracked door to door, including to the USA, UK, Europe, the Middle East and Asia. Import duties and taxes in your country, if any, are payable on delivery.

What is your return policy?

If a stone does not match its description, return it within 14 days of delivery in its original condition, with its report, for a full refund. See our returns policy for details.

How can I pay?

International clients pay by bank transfer or card. In Sri Lanka you can also spread the cost with Koko or Mintpay instalment plans. Payment is only requested after you have seen the stone and agreed the final price.

Can you set a stone into a custom piece of jewellery?

Yes. Our goldsmiths in Sri Lanka handcraft rings, pendants and earrings in gold or silver around the stone you choose. Share your idea on WhatsApp and we will send design sketches and a quote.
